## Adding art

A new picture on the site is a licensing decision. If it is Elusion Studios'
own, it belongs under `images/` and this file already covers it. If it comes
from anyone else - including the Clockwork Raven pack - it gets a section here
under its owner's name, with their credit and website, **before** it is
committed.

If you are an artist and you recognise anything here as yours, please write to
elusionrpg@gmail.com.
