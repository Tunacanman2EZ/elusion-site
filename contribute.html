    Where every dollar goes

    Everything marked for art goes to art.

    Art and music for Elusion, and nothing else — I don't take a wage from the project. A hot cocoa is the one exception: it's a personal thank-you to me, and it is still on the ledger, on its own line, marked personal, because a total that quietly left something out would defeat the point of having one. Anything not yet spent is held in the open for the next piece. If a number ever looks off, email me and I'll walk you through it.
