What's in the world

More than a boss fight — a full run from town, through the portal, and back.

⚔

Four classes

Warrior, Mage, Healer, Tank — melee cleave, ranged burst, ten-shots-a-second spellfire, and a damage aura. Each plays nothing like the others.

♛

Six elemental bosses

The Crowned Behemoth in six flavors, with telegraphed bullet-hell patterns that erupt from the ground. Read them, or get read — and wear armour that turns their element aside.

🎣

A world to live in

Fishing, cooking and pets, with a ten-key hotbar that holds what you carry. The game is more than the fight.

⚖

Trade with anyone

Put items and gold on the table face to face. The kingdom takes a small cut, and Accept only ever agrees to the offer on your screen — no last-second swaps.

🏰

Play together

See everyone in your area walk, fight and bring their pets, live, and take on the same monsters together, each with your own loot. Found a guild, promote your officers, talk in guild chat — every name in the colour its player chose.

🛡

Built to be fair

Every skill, coin and item is server-owned, the server watches every fight, staff have a real moderation desk, and 8,000+ tests run the game as if every player is hostile.

Latest updates

The road to launch, one build at a time. Now on v0.11.4.

Oct 7, 2026v0.11.4

The Credits' Support tab now just asks for a hot cocoa: put your name in the PayPal note and I'll add it to the list.

Oct 7, 2026v0.11.2

Times are right across the clocks changing. In the browser, every chat line, notice and log shows the hour it really happened, even from before a daylight-saving change, and a game left running picks up the new hour when the clocks go forward or back. The Kills window's "counted since" date is your own date now, not the server's. And leap years are checked: Feb 29 is a Tuesday in 2028, and 2100 doesn't have one.

Oct 7, 2026v0.11.1

Credits, under Options. Who drew the art, who made the sound, everyone who has bought me a hot cocoa, and the engine the game is built on — each on its own tab, with links to the artists' own pages.

Oct 7, 2026v0.11.0

Armour resists an element. Every piece of armour now rolls one of the seven lands' elements and a share of its harm to take away — a few percent on iron, more the deeper the tier, and the top of the range on a Perfect piece. Pieces that share an element add up, to half a hit at most, so a set built against fire makes the fire boss a different fight. The shop shows "?" until you buy, the Gear window adds up everything you wear, and weapons never carry an element: what you hit for is still your own.

Oct 7, 2026v0.10.0

The server watches every fight now. Until today it took your game's word that a monster died. Now it keeps its own count of every monster's health from the hits it sees, checks each hit against what your character could really do with the gear and skills you have, and writes down any kill it can't account for. Nothing changes in play yet: kills pay exactly as before. After a week of seeing what honest fights look like, a kill will only pay when the server's count agrees. Also fixed: when you play with others, a large slime no longer shows a second copy of itself for a moment as it splits.

Oct 7, 2026v0.9.0

A kill record. Kills, under Social or on the K key, lists every monster in the game with its picture, how many of each your character has killed and when the last one fell. The Everyone tab adds up every player's kills and shows who has killed the most of each. Monsters you haven't killed yet are greyed out, so it's also a list of what's left to find.

Oct 7, 2026v0.8.0

Bigger text and new fonts. Options can make all the text bigger, up to 150%, and change the font to one of five that suit the game. And you can change your keys: open Controls (H), pick Change keys, click an action and press the key you want. Every action takes two keys, and every key can move except Esc.

Oct 7, 2026v0.7.6

For the downloaded game on a PC with more than one screen: Options has a Window mode (windowed, borderless fullscreen or exclusive fullscreen) and lets you pick which screen to play on, listed by size and refresh rate. The game opens where you left it, and a frame cap can match your screen. If the game stutters on one of your screens, exclusive fullscreen is the one to try.

Oct 6, 2026v0.7.5

Better tools for looking after players. The server keeps the last few snapshots of every character, so if something goes badly wrong with yours I can put it back the way it was. I can also send an item straight to a player, see who's online in a list, and Go to puts me right beside you instead of at the area's entrance.

Oct 6, 2026v0.7.3

Esc closes every window now, including the shop, loot bags, the trade window, the kingdom board and the players list. Closing the trade window still doesn't cancel the trade. And monsters no longer ignore you when you back up against a wall: standing flush against some walls hid you from every enemy in range, so they'd crowd around you and never attack. They see you now, and their shots aim at you rather than at the wall behind you.

Oct 6, 2026v0.7.0

You fight the same monsters now. Everyone in an area sees the same slimes, the same boss and the same spikes coming up out of the floor, and can take them on together. The loot stays yours: when a monster dies, everyone who landed a hit on it gets their own bag and their own XP, and nobody else sees what's in yours. And if someone leaves in the middle of a fight, nothing resets; the monsters carry on right where they were.

Oct 6, 2026v0.6.1

Loot bags can be picked up from much further away. They also stopped catching enemy shots: a bag on the floor used to soak up arrows meant for you. And nobody on staff can create items any more. Only I can, to test things.

Oct 6, 2026v0.6.0

Rare finds wait two days before they can change hands. A mythic, or a Perfect piece from a bag or the shop, can't be traded for 48 hours after you get it. And I can pause trading for everyone with one switch if something looks wrong; a trade that's already open always gets to finish. Trading everything else works exactly as before. It's there so that if someone ever cheats their way to loot, it stays with them while I look.

Oct 6, 2026v0.5.1

In the browser, the little online dots in front of names were showing up as boxes with codes in them, in the Friends, Guild, Trade and Staff windows. The game's font doesn't have those symbols, and a browser has nowhere to borrow them from. They're drawn properly now.

Oct 6, 2026v0.5.0

You can see each other now. Everyone in the same area shows up on your screen, live: walking and turning, swinging their weapon, the tank's aura lit, their pet trailing behind, and their name over their head in the colour they chose, with their guild and the crown or staff badge. It's company, not shared combat yet. Each of you still fights your own monsters, so a friend swinging at nothing is swinging at something only they can see. Shared fights are next.

Oct 5, 2026

Gear rolls its stats now. Every piece that drops, and every piece you buy, rolls each of its stats between 85% and 115% of normal, and one in a hundred is Perfect: 120% on everything, in a gold frame. The shop shows a "?" where the stats go, and you find out what you got when you buy it. Two jade swords aren't the same sword any more.

Oct 5, 2026

You can sell to the shop. It pays a twentieth of what a piece is worth: enough to clear out a bag, never enough to beat hunting. Prices went up across the board too. Gear costs twice what it did and potions four times, so a potion now costs about what the cooked fish of its tier is worth.

Oct 5, 2026

The three spike gates by the ladder down to the bosses have levers now, one on each side. Pull either one and all three drop; pull it again and they rise. And going through the town portal no longer plays its sound twice.

Oct 4, 2026

You can play Elusion in your browser now, at play.elusionrpg.com. Your characters live on the game's own server, not in your browser, so they're there whenever you sign in again. Bring a keyboard and mouse.

Oct 2, 2026

Every enemy in the game can now drop a mythic weapon, and the tougher it is, the better your odds. The Crowned is about one in 150 kills. A light slime is one in two million, a real lottery ticket. It's always your own class's weapon. When one drops, the bag lands under a tall red pillar of light and the screen flashes red, and everyone online gets a banner saying who found it and on what. The bag stays on the ground for five minutes, so a boss fight can't cost you it.

Oct 2, 2026

Three new weapons from Ahvassa, and each one changes how your class attacks. The mage's Meteorite brings a meteor down where you aim. It comes in at an angle, its shadow grows until it lands, and it leaves a crater. The warrior's Double Axe is thrown to a spot and spins there, cutting everything near it, until you call it back. The tank's Dynamite replaces the aura with a lit stick on a fuse. One meteor or throw in ten comes twice. They need level 22.

Oct 1, 2026

I played Elusion as a brand-new player for the first time, and it was rough. Nothing told me that Space attacks, Shift sprints or E opens the bank, nothing said which way the Field was, and the bar along the bottom was fourteen tiny buttons. Now the first time you stand in town, a welcome card shows you the way out and every key, and H brings the keys back whenever you want them. The bar is down to eight: Friends, Players, Guild, Trade and the Kingdom board live under Social, and Options and logging out under Menu.

Oct 1, 2026

A round of polish. The bank's Deposit and Withdraw, the level on Character Stats and all eight gear slots now use the same sockets as the hotbar, so the whole game reads as one set. The cooking window used to open stuck in the top-left corner with twelve empty boxes; it opens in the middle now, with one row of six, one per kind of fish. And Character Stats is two columns with short numbers: 1M, not 1,007,892.

Sep 29, 2026

Trading got a proper overhaul, and testing it with two accounts found the nasty one: whoever pressed Accept first could lose the item they'd just traded for, the next time they so much as moved a potion. Their game never heard the trade had finished, so it saved the old bag over the new one. Fixed. Accept now only agrees to the offer you actually saw, so nobody can swap a sword for a stick at the last second. You're told when someone wants to trade with you, items show their icons and worth, and your recent trades are listed.

Sep 29, 2026

Guild chat works. The tab said "Guilds are not in the game yet" long after guilds were in the game — the server had guild chat the whole time, and the game never asked it. A player typed "oi bruv" into it, nothing happened, and that was the bug report.

Sep 29, 2026

My mods have a proper desk now: every player's record, a log of every ban, kick and warning, and staff-only notes, so whoever is on can pick up where the last person left off when I'm not around. When somebody says they got scammed, staff can look up exactly what was traded, with whom, and on which character.

Sep 29, 2026

The name colour you pick in Options finally shows everywhere — chat, friends, guilds and the players list, not just over your own head. Rank became a badge instead (the crown, MOD, DEV), because once colours are free, a colour can't prove anyone is staff. Guilds got a cleaner panel too: online members first, what everyone is playing, and a short history of who joined, got promoted or left.

Sep 27, 2026

I died, came back with full health, logged out, and logged back in on 52 HP out of 504. My own server had been catching it for weeks and I hadn't looked — the client was healing itself, and the check that watches for unexplained healing was trimming it back to what regeneration could really produce. It was right. Accepting death was still a decision made on the player's machine rather than a request the server had to agree to. Both ways out of the death screen go through the server now, and the gold you were carrying is properly destroyed rather than just vanishing off your screen and coming back at the next login.

Sep 27, 2026

A switch that makes me hostile. Flip it and the whole world is told — and it stays on screen for as long as it's true, rather than scrolling away like any other message. There's also a Players menu anyone can open: who is online, what they're playing, and where they are. One day I want to be the world boss.

Sep 27, 2026

Every line of chat and every notice from the server now says when it happened, in your own local time. That sounds small. It wasn't: the server had been sending the time all along and the game was throwing it away, so anyone logging in was handed a week of old announcements, every one of them claiming to be happening right now.

Sep 27, 2026

The Kingdom's Coffers counts every gold piece that leaves the world for good — and it now says how much of a player's contribution was given and how much was simply lost when they died. Deaths are counted too. Dying still climbs the board; the board just admits what it's made of.

Sep 23, 2026

Elusion is getting a sound. It took me over a year of asking to get Horizon into my Discord — he's 17, has been writing music since he was 15, plays football, and is a genuinely busy guy. He's working on the intro theme in secret and has assured me it's no problem. A placeholder is in for now; the real track is on the way.

Sep 23, 2026

Every skill is now server-owned. Attack, defense, agility, magic, fishing and cooking are granted and checked on the server — no client can claim or edit its way to level 99.

Sep 23, 2026

Security pass across the board: saves, inventory, gold and the economy are all reconciled against what the server actually granted, backed by 3,400+ automated tests.
