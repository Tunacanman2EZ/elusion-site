The game

What's in the world

More than a boss fight — a full run from town, through the portal, and back.

Four classes

Warrior, Mage, Healer and Tank — each plays nothing like the others.

Bullet hell and seven bosses

Weave through every monster's bullets, then face the Crowned Beholder in all seven elements.

A world to live in

Fishing, cooking, a smith and pets — more than the fight.

Trade with anyone

Items and gold, face to face, and Accept only ever agrees to the offer on your screen.

Play together

Fight the same monsters live, each with your own loot, in a party with chat and voice, or a guild.

Built to be fair

Every coin and item is the server's, and 11,000+ tests treat every player as hostile.

The world

Seven elements, gentlest to deepest

Every monster past the portal belongs to one of seven elements, from light, the gentlest, to dark, the deepest. Each has its own patch of the Field, and each pays back what it costs you — the deeper the element, the better the gear.

Owner log

The latest pushes

The road to launch, one build at a time. Now on v0.93.0.

Oct 27, 2026v0.93.0

Every element now fights its own way everywhere, not just in the Field. In the Big Field and the crypt, light monsters fire prism rings, wind sweeps gale fans, water sends a slow wall with a gap to slip through, ice leaves frost mines where you stood, earth lobs boulders, fire closes a pincer on you, and dark picks any of them. Watch the pattern and find the gap. And chat is easy to walk with again: the Chat button opens it see-through so you can keep moving, and Enter opens it to type. Trading is easier too: when someone asks to trade, a card pops up with See offer and No thanks. The trade window is smaller and opens beside your bag, which opens with it. Double-click an item in your bag (or hold it on a phone, or drag it across) to give it, use - and + for how many, and the columns now read You give and You get. And there is somewhere new to go: a ladder in the town square leads down to The Endless. Open the door at the end of the corridor and step through, and the slimes start coming, a little tougher every minute, thousands of them, with that land's elites and every few minutes its boss. See how long you last. Falling there is safe: you wake in the corridor with everything you had.

Oct 27, 2026v0.92.0

A friendlier first few minutes. A new character now gets a gold pointer that shows the way: to the portal into the town square, then the portal out to the Big Field, then your first enemy. It goes away after your first kill. Every new character also starts with five health potions on hotbar key 1 (mages and healers get five mana potions on key 2 as well), and the welcome card now tells you the shop gives most classes a free mythic weapon.

Oct 27, 2026v0.91.3

On a phone, the Use button now stays on screen beside your health and mana, and the Menu button sits further in from the corner, so it is no longer cut off.
